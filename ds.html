06 / BRAND IDENTITY SPEC

Logo Usage & Isolation Zone

Guidelines for proper brand logo placement. The official wordmark must always have an isolation safety zone equal to 1x height of the 'L' character on all sides. No graphic elements or text should enter this clear space.

Rules for Application & Brand Integrity

  • Preferred Placement: The silver-on-dark logo should always be centered on dark charcoal (#2A2D35) or near-black (#1A1C22) backgrounds to preserve contrast and stage presence.
  • Minimum Width: On desktop formats, the logo should never be displayed smaller than 240px wide to preserve the legibility of the sub-eyebrow "DJ & Electronic Music Producer".
  • Color Integrity: Never apply intense rainbow gradients or unbranded neon colors onto the main wordmark. Maintain the refined, textured silver gradients.
